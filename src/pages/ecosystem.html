Business models that work

When infrastructure is cheap, you don't need to sell your users' attention to survive.

❌ The traditional model

→ Provide a "free" product to attract users
→ Monetize via advertising: sell access to user attention
→ Maximize engagement to maximize ad impressions
→ Engagement optimization = manipulation, addiction, outrage
→ Users have no choice — there's only one platform

✓ What SPXP enables

→ Paid client apps — buy once, no tracking required. Like email clients.
→ Subscription hosting — pay for space, no ads. Like web hosting.
→ Donation-based clients — open source apps funded by community.
→ Enterprise hosting — organizations with SLAs, compliance, support.
→ Users can switch freely — real market competition on merit
💡

The key insight

An SPXP profile is a static JSON file. Hosting costs pennies. When your infrastructure doesn't cost millions to run, you don't need to monetize attention to survive. Advertising isn't banned — it's just no longer forced.

For the first time, users have genuine choice: pick a client with no ads, pay for hosting that doesn't track you, or build your own. The market can compete on real value.
